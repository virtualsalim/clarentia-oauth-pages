Privacy Policy

Clarentia Backup is a private administrative utility used to store Clarentia system backups in an authorized Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The application requests Google Drive access solely to create, read, update, and manage Clarentia backup files in the authorized account. Google Drive data is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Data sharing

Google user data accessed by Clarentia Backup is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data retention

Backup files are retained according to Clarentia's administrative backup procedures and remain under the control of the authorized Google account owner.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security and connected-app settings. Revoking access prevents future Google Drive operations by Clarentia Backup.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
